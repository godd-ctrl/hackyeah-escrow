Get paid for work, without trusting a platform to hold the money.

A client locks the job's full amount in an on-chain vault. The freelancer sees the money exists before starting. The client releases it milestone by milestone. If the client goes silent, the freelancer can claim everything after the deadline. No platform holds the funds, and no party can change the terms unilaterally — the rules live in the program.

Who this is for

A freelancer taking a first job from a stranger overseas, and a small client who does not want to wire money to someone they met yesterday. Today they use an Upwork-style platform, which holds the funds, takes a cut, and can freeze either side's account without explanation.

What the intermediary was doing

Holding money in the middle, deciding who was right in a dispute, and enforcing the schedule. The first two are replaced by a vault only the program can move plus deterministic rules; the third by a deadline that lets the freelancer claim the remaining balance if the client never reviews.

What changes without it

The client cannot unilaterally take the money back after work has started. The freelancer cannot take it before delivery. Neither needs an account, a reputation score, or a permission to be paid. The platform's fee and its discretion both disappear, and the transaction is the agreement.
